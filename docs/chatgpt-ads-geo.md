# ChatGPT Ads — English campaign locations (10 Sep 2026)

**In one line:** one English campaign, max $3 per click, three ad groups (US / UK / Canada wording). Locations: `United Kingdom` + `Ontario, Canada` + `Quebec, Canada` + `Nova Scotia, Canada` + `Florida, United States` + `Texas, United States` + `New York, United States` (the state row) + `Georgia, United States`. Re-tier after ~300 clicks or 4 weeks, on booked calls rather than click-through.

**Reading the tables.** "Type into picker" gives the recommended name first, then alternatives from coarse to fine (US rows: state → Market → two ZIP codes). The official location file (468,271 rows, downloaded 10 Sep 2026 [GEO]) has exactly four kinds of row: Country, Region, Market (US TV markets, listed in the file as "TV Region") and Postal Code. There is no city, county, borough or metro row in any country, so every city name from earlier drafts is gone. What exists: US = 57 states/territories, 210 Markets, 41,488 ZIPs (`33131, Miami, Florida, United States`); UK = `England`, `Scotland`, `Wales`, `Northern Ireland` plus 2,979 postcode districts (`N1, Barnsbury, ENG, United Kingdom` — the town shown is often a neighbourhood); Canada = 13 provinces/territories plus 1,652 three-letter postal prefixes (`M5V, Toronto, ON, Canada`); France = 13 regions plus 6,064 postal codes (`31000, Toulouse, France`). Markets show as `<name>, United States`; `New York, United States` is the same string for the Market and the state, so pick by the type label. Always read the country suffix and type label before adding a row: Surrey, Cambridge, London, Halifax, Victoria and Hamilton exist in two of the three countries, "Austin" also matches `Rochester - Mason City - Austin` (Minnesota), "Manchester" surfaces `Boston (Manchester)`, and the search box rejects some spellings (`München` works, `Munich` does not [MCP]). Sources are keyed [XX] and listed at the end. Time offsets are for September; Paris leaves summer time on 25 Oct, North America on 1 Nov, so US and Canada offsets are 1 h smaller during that week (the UK moves with Paris).

- **Seattle – Tacoma** — 4.1k stores [SL], −9 h; revisit after month 1.
- **Boston** (`Boston (Manchester)`) — lowest new-business rate of the states compared (1,126 per 100k [LT]); biotech audience; 4.2k stores [SL].
- **San Diego** — Pacific hours and California prices for a quarter of LA's pool (7.7k stores [SL][SA]); `California, United States` covers it if the West is ever added.
- **Wyoming, Delaware, Nevada** (18.6k "stores" in Sheridan, WY [SL]) — registered-agent addresses, not local demand.
- **Belfast / Northern Ireland** — 961 stores [SLUK]; the least online-shopping UK region.
- **Newcastle / North East** — least business-dense English region (744 per 10,000 adults [BPE]); 1.1k stores, a fifth of Manchester [SLUK].
- **Cambridge, Oxford** — deep-tech and biotech founders; 663 and 382 stores [SLUK].
- **Montréal** — no longer avoided: it comes in with `Quebec, Canada` (Canada table).
- **Québec City** — 97% French-first [SC5]; sits inside the `Quebec, Canada` pick and adds almost nothing to the English campaign; nothing to type.
- **Gatineau** — French-majority [SC1]; also inside the Quebec pick. If you ever drop Quebec, `Ontario, Canada` excludes it automatically; there is no `Ottawa, Ontario` city row, only prefixes such as `K1P, Ottawa, ON, Canada`.
- **Winnipeg, Hamilton, London (Ontario)** — no distinct store or founder signal [SLCA][SC7]; Ontario covers the last two anyway.

## Structure + budget
- **One campaign, eight locations, states not Markets.** Budget is set per campaign (US minimum reported at $25/day; daily spend is a 7-day average with up to 2x on one day [OA1][SOKU][PPC]) and locations attach to the campaign, not to ad groups [GEO]. Phase-1 list: `United Kingdom` + `Ontario, Canada` + `Quebec, Canada` + `Nova Scotia, Canada` + `Florida, United States` + `Texas, United States` + `New York, United States` (state row) + `Georgia, United States`. The arithmetic behind it: the four Markets (New York 5.97%, Atlanta 2.20%, Miami 1.40%, Austin 0.82% of US TV homes [NLS]) = 10.4% of the US × 17.2% (US share of ChatGPT traffic [SIANA]) = 1.8% of all chats, against 4.3% for the UK — so with Markets the UK, not the US, would take most clicks. The four states are 25.2% of the US population (85.7M of 340.1M [CEN]) × 17.2% = 4.3%, level with the UK; Ontario + Quebec + Nova Scotia = 63% of Canadians [SC6] × 2.6% = 1.6%. Expected pool before prices ≈ 42% US / 42% UK / 16% Canada (estimate); US search clicks cost about twice UK/Canada ones on Google (US $7.66, UK $3.47, CA $3.05, Dec 2024 [AB]), so the $3 cap will push the US share below that. One rule everywhere: phase 1 = Eastern/Central/Atlantic time only; Alberta, British Columbia, California, Colorado and Utah join together in phase 2.
- **Three ad groups, measured by country in Umami.** Ad groups carry US / UK / Canada wording. Hints match the conversation, not the location, so a "sole trader" hint can fire in Toronto: ad-group figures are a vocabulary proxy, not a country report, and reporting by location is unconfirmed [OA3]. Give each ad group its own tracking tag (utm_campaign=chatgpt-en, utm_content=us / uk / ca), then read visitor country in Umami on the /en/ landing pages and /en/diagnostic starts — that gives clicks and diagnostic starts per country whatever the Ads Manager shows.
- **Bid and budget.** Max $3 per click, the bottom of OpenAI's $3–5 guidance [OA2]; a cap does not make clicks cheaper — in pricier US auctions it means fewer US impressions, not cheaper ones. €18–20/day per campaign (EN and FR) reaches ~€500 in 14 days, reportedly what unlocks the matching $500 credit, which expires after 90 days [PROMO]; €15/day falls short. If the account minimum turns out to be $25/day, run at the minimum: one campaign alone reaches ~€500 in 14 days (14 × ~€22). What €20/day buys: at $3–5 a click, 4–7 clicks a day, 30–45 a week, 130–200 a month (arithmetic); one published 15-day report shows $1.72 a click and 2.35% conversion, swinging day to day [OPA]; typical click-through ~0.9% [TRU]; e-commerce clicks $3–5, software $8–18 [WFX]. To judge anything you need ~300 clicks and ~7 conversions per variant over at least a week [ADL] — 6–10 weeks per lesson at this rate, enough for one campaign and one main ad group, not for city comparisons.
- **When to widen, narrow or split.** Widen when the campaign spends under budget three days running (add `Alberta, Canada` and `British Columbia, Canada`, then `California` / `Colorado` / `Utah`), or after ~300 clicks or 4 weeks with an acceptable lead rate (add the rest of the US as its own campaign). Narrow when one country takes over 70% of clicks while another gets under 5 a week: if the UK dominates, add `Illinois, United States` and `North Carolina, United States` (Raleigh is the #4 large metro for small business [CW]); if the US dominates, drop `Texas` first. If the US gets under 5 clicks a week after two weeks, raise the cap to $4 before adding locations. Split into one campaign per country when each can carry $25–30/day or click prices differ by more than ~50% between countries: UK campaign first (cheapest clicks, best hours, UK case studies), US next, Canada folded into the UK one or alone.
- **Calls and policy.** Open /en/book slots 15:00–21:00 Paris = UK all day, Atlantic 10:00–16:00, Eastern 09:00–15:00, Central 08:00–14:00; show times in the visitor's zone. West Coast (phase 2): written or video updates plus 1–2 slots at 18:00–20:00 Paris. Frame the ads as a product ("AI assistant for your online shop", "automated quotes and follow-ups", "e-commerce security check") with "from €500" visible on the /en/ pages; no "local", "near you", "#1", "guaranteed", "risk-free", no negative competitor mentions; B2B/agency is not in the early-phase focus list, so a rejection is possible [OAP][ABM][CEK].

## Québec (French campaign)
- Locations attach to the campaign, not the ad group. To reach Québec inside the FR campaign, add `Quebec, Canada` to that campaign's location list (never plain `Canada`, which also pulls English Canada) and add a Québec-worded ad group. Every ad group in that campaign can then serve in both France and Québec — accept that, or run Québec as its own campaign once it can carry the minimum daily budget. Timing: phase 2, once the France ad group has ~300 clicks.
- Names that exist [GEO]: `Quebec, Canada` (Region; the file spells it without the accent); postal prefixes `H2X, Plateau Mont-Royal, QC, Canada`, `H2Y, Montreal, QC, Canada` (city hall), `H3B, Montreal, QC, Canada`, `G1R, Capitale-Nationale, QC, Canada` (Québec City), `J8X, Hull, QC, Canada` (Gatineau). No city rows. If a search box is used, try both "Montréal" and "Montreal", "Québec" and "Quebec City" — the search rejects some spellings [MCP].
- Worth it: 228,622 small employer businesses, 21.2% of Canada [ISED]; the Montréal metro has 139,798 employer businesses [SC7] and holds 50.1% of Québec's employer enterprises [ISQ]; French is the first official language of 77% in the Montréal area [SC4]. Real hook: since 1 June 2025, Law 14 (Bill 96) requires French parity for commercial websites and any chat or assistant, fines $3,000–$30,000 [CFIB][WEG] — "votre assistant IA doit répondre en français" is factual and compliant.
- Wording swaps: devis → soumission; e-mail → courriel; auto-entrepreneur → travailleur autonome; chat → clavardage; newsletter → infolettre; TVA → TPS/TVQ; e-commerce → commerce en ligne / boutique en ligne [OQLF].
- Remove "on se déplace", Ariège/Occitanie, "près de chez vous", SIRET. Say "livraison à distance, appels vidéo 15 h–19 h heure de Paris = 9 h–13 h heure de Montréal". Keep prices in € ("facturé en euros, hors taxes, paiement par carte"); an indicative CAD figure on the landing page only.
- Landing page: a /fr/quebec variant or a Québec section; the FR diagnostic must not assume French tax or legal context.
- Inventory is small (Canada ≈ 2.6% of ChatGPT traffic, estimate [SIANA]): the risk is too few clicks to read, not overspend.
